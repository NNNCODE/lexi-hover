Kenning

A hover dictionary for people learning English, that opens where you are reading.

Hold Alt and point at any English word on a web page. A small card opens on the spot with the pronunciation, the part of speech, a short English definition, an example and a translation into the language you choose. Move on and it closes.

What it does

  • Underlines the words you are likely not to know, graded by frequency or by exam syllabus, from a dictionary bundled inside the extension.
  • Draws a short meaning above each underlined word, if you want it (in Chinese, for now).
  • Tells you before you read a page how many of its words are above your level.
  • Translates a selected phrase with one press, and keeps a sentence for later without translating it.
  • Keeps a word list with the sentence each word was met in, and reviews it on a spaced schedule.
  • Saves a page, a PDF or a YouTube transcript to read later, with everything above.
  • Shows a translation under each paragraph, in your language, using the browser's own on-device translator.

What leaves your browser

  • The one word you looked up, sent to a public dictionary and a public translation service without cookies.
  • Nothing else — not the page, not its address, not your word list. No account, no analytics.
  • Two optional features send more, and each is off until you set it up with credentials of your own.

Read the privacy policy — it is short.
