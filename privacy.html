Lexi Hover — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-27. Applies to Lexi Hover version 0.7.0 and later.

Lexi Hover is a browser extension that shows a dictionary card when you hold Alt and point at an English word, underlines words you may not know, and keeps a word list on your device. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own.

The short version

  • Nothing about you is collected. No account, no e-mail, no identifier, no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • A single word leaves your browser when you look one up. It goes to a public dictionary service and a public translation service, and to nothing else.
  • Everything you save stays on your device — your word list, your saved sentences, your reading list, your study history and your settings.
  • Two optional features send more, and each one is off until you set it up yourself with something only you can provide: a server you deployed, or an API credential of your own.

What leaves your browser, and when

WhatWhenWhere it goes
One English wordYou hold Alt and point at itapi.dictionaryapi.dev (definitions, pronunciation) and api.mymemory.translated.net (Chinese translation)

A pronunciation audio fileYou press the speaker buttonThe address the dictionary service returned
The surrounding sentence, as context for a lookupOnly if you turn Send sentence for context on (off by default)DeepL, as a context parameter, if you use DeepL

Every request is made without cookies and without a referrer, so the services above receive the word and nothing about which page you were reading.

Results are cached on your device for 30 days, so a word the dictionary service has answered before makes no request at all. A word it failed to answer may be asked for again later.

What never leaves your browser

  • The pages you visit, their addresses, their titles or their content.
  • Your browsing history.
  • Your word list, saved sentences, reading list and study history — unless you turn on sync, described below.
  • Any word the extension underlines. Underlining is graded against a dictionary bundled inside the extension; it reads every word on the page and transmits none of them.
  • Paragraph translation, which uses your browser's own on-device translator and has no online fallback.
  • The sentence you translate with the 文A button on a word card while no AI credentials are saved (versions after 0.7.0): that button then uses the same on-device translator.
  • A word you type into Look up a word in the toolbar popup (versions after 0.7.0). It is answered from the dictionary bundled inside the extension and nothing else, so a word that dictionary lacks is reported as not found rather than sent anywhere.

The two optional features that send more

Both are off when you install the extension, and neither can be turned on by accident: each needs you to paste something into the extension's settings.

Sync. If you deploy a sync server of your own and paste its address into the word list page, your word list is sent to that server encrypted in your browser with a key derived from a code that never leaves your device. The server stores an opaque id, a version number and bytes it cannot read. There is no account; whoever holds your code can read the list, and nobody can recover it for you. Sync runs about a minute after you file a word and every half hour otherwise, or only when you press Sync now if you turn the automatic option off.

Sentence translation by an AI model (the 文A button on a word card). If you paste a Cloudflare account id and an API token of your own into the popup, pressing that button sends the word and the one sentence it appeared in — nothing else — to api.cloudflare.com (Workers AI), under Cloudflare's terms. The sentence is capped at 400 characters. The token is sent in a request header, stored only on your device, and never given to any web page. The answer is kept in memory while the browser is open and discarded when it closes. Without these credentials the same button translates the sentence on your device and sends nothing (versions after 0.7.0; in 0.7.0 the button is not shown without them).

What is stored on your device

All of it is in the browser's extension storage, which is removed when you uninstall:

  • Settings, including any API key or sync code you entered.
  • Your word list, with up to two sentences per word and the page each came from. Page addresses are stored as origin and path only — the query string and fragment, which is where session tokens and tracking ids live, are dropped before anything is written.
  • Sentences you kept, with the page's origin, path and title.
  • Pages and documents you saved to the reading list (text only, no images or scripts).
  • Your study log: how many words were filed and graded on each day, and nothing else.
  • Lookup results, for 30 days.

The extension deliberately does not use the browser's own account-backed sync storage.

Third parties

The services named above receive the words you look up under their own privacy terms: dictionaryapi.dev, MyMemory, and, only if you set them up, DeepL and Cloudflare. Lexi Hover does not sell, share or otherwise use any data for purposes other than the feature you triggered.

Children

The extension does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version sends anything new anywhere, this policy and the extension's own README change in the same release, and the version number at the top of this page is updated.
